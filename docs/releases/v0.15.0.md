## Activity detection v2, app rules, and detection accuracy

**Detection that reacts at once.** Dynamic power saving now uses a new activity detector that weighs many signals and gives each activity a confidence:
- **Instant signals:** typing, clicking, and scrolling (raw input, timing only) and program switches re-run the governor immediately instead of at the next one-second check. Moving the pointer alone no longer triggers the boost.
- **What is in front:** the foreground program is categorised (browser, documents, development, chat, media, creative/compute, game), and BatteryScope checks whether it fills the screen, including borderless full screen.
- **Screen and GPU:** the compositor frame rate tells whether anything on screen is moving. GPU 3D, compute, and video-decode use is read every 2 seconds, so muted video and borderless games are recognised.
- **Sound, calls, lock:** sound is traced to the program playing it. A locked screen or display off always counts as idle.
- **Confidence:** moves toward performance happen at once, even right after a saving change. Moves toward saving wait longer when the detector is less sure.

**App rules.** In Settings → App rules, choose per program: always save power, always balanced, performance first, or leave alone. When you or G-Helper move a setting the same way twice in the same program, BatteryScope learns a rule for that program, which you can change or forget.

**Detection accuracy in the automatic test.** While the test runs, detection is scored against the activity actually running. The accuracy goes into the export summary, with `detection.csv` and a per-second `detection-log.csv`.

**Status page.** The dynamic saving status page shows the detected activity, its confidence and reasons, other candidates, and the foreground program with its rule. Nothing is captured from the screen, and no keys are recorded.

Not yet verified on real hardware: touchpad raw input, the GPU counter values on this laptop, and how learning interacts with G-Helper's automatic switching. The automatic test's `detection-log.csv` will be used to tune the thresholds.
