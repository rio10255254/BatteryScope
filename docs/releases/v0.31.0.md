## Effects: light that leaves the panel

0.31 makes the keycap panel move, and lets its light go beyond its own window. Everything is event-driven: something moves only when you (or the charger) do something, and a panel at rest still repaints nothing.

**Beyond the window**

- **Opening.** Sparks rise from the tray icon to the panel, the panel's light blooms round it, and a band of light sweeps up the panel as it powers on.
- **Closing.** The light runs back into the tray icon and lands with a ring.
- **Day and night.** Changing the theme wipes the old look away in a circle from where you clicked. A ring of the new light runs on its edge, past the panel.
- **Switches, modes and tabs.** A switch turning on, a new performance mode and a new tab each throw dot-matrix sparks and a ring of light; some fly past the panel's edge.
- **The screen's edge.**
  - Charger in: two lights run from the bottom centre round the screen, meet at the top, and the border glows and fades.
  - Charger out: the lit border drains back down.
  - Performance mode changed by hand (Fn key or the panel): a burst along the bottom edge. Silent is short and cool, balanced is neutral, turbo is wide and warm.
  - Low battery: two red pulses. Charge reminder: one pulse in the panel's light.
- **The mascot.** Click it: it hops and throws sparks up past the top of the panel.

**Inside the panel**

- **A display on the home page.** The Performance tab opens with the power in dot-matrix digits and the battery as a percentage over ten cells.
- **Digits that behave like a display.** Numbers count up when a page opens. A changed value scans in column by column behind a lit column.
- **Light under the pointer.** A key under the pointer catches a pool of light that follows it, and its shadow lengthens.
- **Press ripple.** A pressed key sends a ring of light out from the press.
- **LED meters.** The leading LED flares while a value is on the move; lit LEDs share one glow.
- **Charging.** A light marches up the empty battery cells with every sample.

**When it does not happen**

- Settings → Appearance → Effects: Full (default), Panel only, or Off.
- Effects beyond the window are skipped in battery saver and with a contrast theme. The screen-edge light is also skipped over a full-screen game, a presentation, or in quiet hours.
- With Windows animations off, nothing moves at all.

**How it is drawn**

- Effects beyond the window are drawn in software into click-through, always-on-top layered windows that exist only while something moves. They never take focus or clicks, and their bitmaps are freed afterwards.
- A key, with its shadow, light and edges, is now one bitmap worked out once per state and then only copied (it was drawn with GDI+ every time). Measured in Wine at 96 DPI, a full page takes about 2 ms; before this change the keycap style took 4.6–6.3 ms.

建議：先試「設定 → 外觀 → 主題」切換深淺色、插拔充電器、按 Fn 切效能模式這三個最明顯的特效；覺得太花可以在「設定 → 外觀 → 動態特效」改成「只在面板內」或「關閉」。
