## A calmer panel, light that is never cut, motion in step with the display

0.32 answers two complaints: the panel looked rough, and the keycap style had turned everything into a button. Every page was checked at 200% scaling, the scale of the G14's display, before anything was changed.

**Fewer buttons**

- **Rows lie flat.** Setting rows, program rows and filters are no longer keycaps. They lie on the panel with a dotted line between them, and a row rises as a key only under the pointer.
- **One lit key per group.** The tabs and the performance modes each lie in a tray. Only the chosen one is a lit key, and it glides to the new choice.
- **Keys mean "press me now".** A command that can be used is a key. One that cannot is an empty outline.

**Rough edges removed**

- **Light and shadow are no longer cut into rectangles.** The glow of a lit key and the shadow under a key used to stop dead at the edge of their window. They now thin out to nothing before the edge.
- **No Windows scroll bar.** The program list has the panel's own slim indicator, which shows while scrolling or when the pointer is near, and can be dragged. Program rows are now as wide as the buttons above them.
- **The header lines up.** The power mode key sits level with the line of text under the name.
- **A softer press.** The ring that spread from a press was a hard arc. It is now a soft wave of light.
- **The lab's 95% range** no longer crosses the labels: bright LEDs end at the average, dimmer ones on either side show the range.
- **The display** shows its unlit dots faintly behind the lit ones. Section labels are followed by a dotted rule.

**Motion in step with the display**

- Animation was driven by window timers, which tick every 15.6 ms and drift against the screen. On a 120 Hz panel that shows as judder.
- Now, only while something moves, a thread waits for each frame of the display and every animation steps on it: 120 steps a second at 120 Hz, 60 at 60 Hz. At rest nothing runs.

**New motion and feedback**

- **Page titles decode.** A new page's title flickers through other characters and settles from the left.
- **Section rules run out** from their labels behind a lit head as a page opens.
- **The page gives at its ends.** Scroll past the top or bottom and it yields a little, then springs back.
- **The mascot watches the pointer.**
- **The tray icon answers.** Charger in: it fills three times. Charger out: it drains. A performance mode chosen by hand (Fn key or the panel): one, two or three bars. Then the percentage comes back.
- **The key display's bar glides** to a new level when a key is pressed again.

Tray icon shows follow Settings → Appearance → Effects (Full only), like the other effects beyond the panel.

建議：先看「設定」與「硬體」兩頁（變化最大），再切換幾次分頁與效能模式看亮鍵滑動；插拔一次充電器看系統匣圖示。
