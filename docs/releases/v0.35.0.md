## CPU power no longer held at the base clock

A check of what holds the CPU's power down, and three fixes.

- **Plugged in, balanced.** BatteryScope set CPU Boost to off for the balanced mode, which keeps the CPU at its base clock: about 18–20 W under full load on the G14. Balanced now uses **efficient enabled**: no boost in light use, boost when a load lasts. The menu offers it for every mode. A balanced setting stored as "off" is moved once; what you choose afterwards is kept.
- **On battery, automatic mode.** Heavy work and bursts switched to balanced but left Boost "as it was", and with Boost off in the plan they stayed at the base clock. They now switch Boost on themselves.
- **Extra saving.** Being behind the battery target, or the battery being low, no longer throttles heavy work that is running. Only a very low battery does.

The firmware's own limit for the balanced mode is unchanged.
