## A clean, macOS-like look; the pixel style stays as an option

The panel now looks and moves like a macOS app by default. The pixel style is unchanged and one click away in Settings → Style.

**Look**

- macOS system colours: window grey, a lighter content area, white grouped rows (graphite in dark mode), label and secondary-label greys, and the system blue accent. Warnings and errors use the system orange and red.
- Settings rows that follow one another form one rounded list with hairline separators, as in System Settings. A row's value is plain grey text beside the chevron.
- Section titles are plain bold labels; page titles are larger and bold, without a rule under them.
- Switches are capsules that fill with the accent, with a white knob and a soft shadow.
- The performance mode and the tab bar are segmented controls: a raised thumb on a grey track.
- Menus highlight the row in the accent colour with white text and mark the chosen option with a check.
- Meters are thinner, tags are capsules, and the battery mascot (a pixel sprite) shows in the pixel style only.

**Motion, learned frame by frame from a reference clip**

A reference animation was studied frame by frame (at 60 fps, measuring brightness and glow per frame). Its rules now drive the clean style's motion:

- **Two speeds.** A change moves quickly and evenly: about 150 ms, ease-in-out, no overshoot. Its detail then settles more slowly over 300–450 ms.
- **Lift.** Whatever travels (a switch knob, the performance mode's thumb, the tab bar's thumb) lifts a little on the way, with a deeper shadow, and settles as it lands.
- **Light on landing.** As it lands, a soft halo in the accent colour blooms and fades. A light runs a quarter turn along its edge from the side it came from.
- **Anticipation.** A pressed switch's knob stretches before it moves.
- **Labels.** A segment's label brightens as the thumb arrives under it, not before.
- **Values.** A changed value dissolves and re-forms. The old one rises and fades in about 120 ms, and the new one comes up from below, lit in the accent, and settles to its colour.
- **Pages.** On opening, a page's rows move into place quickly (170 ms), and their colours settle in over 360 ms, slightly staggered.
- **Light and dark.** Changing the theme crossfades every colour of the panel in about 160 ms, as quick as the clip's own change. Text switches at the midpoint, so it is never lost against a background passing through grey.

All motion still stops when Windows is set to reduce animations.

Updating switches to the new style once; choose Pixel in Settings → Style to go back.

建議：更新後介面會換成新的簡約風格；想用原本的像素風格，到「設定 → 外觀 → 風格」切回「像素」即可。
