## Saving where the battery's power really goes: the display

The lab's trace from the G14 (about six hours on battery, every two seconds) shows how much dynamic saving already does. At idle, at the same 120 Hz on the same day, the battery drew 9.4 W with it and 11.4 W without, with the CPU at 2.6 W against 4.6 W. That 2.6 W is close to the floor for this CPU. Most of what is left is not the CPU: display, discrete GPU, storage and network took 6.8–9 W of every 9–12 W. The power plan also had the display never turning off on battery.

**Dim and black out when away (battery only)**

With nobody at the PC, the display dims to 30% after 1 minute and turns black after 5 minutes. All of this is adjustable on the Saving tab:

- **Dim after:** off, 30 s, or 1, 2 or 5 min
- **Black after:** never, or 2, 5, 10, 15 or 30 min
- **Dimmed brightness:** 10–50%

How it works:

- **Dimming.** This is Windows' own timer on the active power plan, for battery only. Windows keeps the display bright while a program asks it to (a video, a presentation, a call). The first touch brings the brightness back.
- **Black screen.** BatteryScope covers the screen with black, which turns an OLED panel's pixels off. The PC stays awake and the network stays connected. The first key, click or mouse move lifts it at once, and that key or click does not reach the program below.
- **Why not Windows' own display-off.** On a Modern Standby laptop like the G14, it also starts standby and drops the network. BatteryScope leaves that setting as you have it.
- **When it does not go black:**
  - With dynamic saving on, only when its detector says idle. The detector weighs sound, video decoding, full screen and calls.
  - With dynamic saving off, not while sound is playing, during a call, or with a full-screen app.
  - Never while a program asks Windows to keep the display on.
  - Never when plugged in, during a check or a lab test, or in protection mode.
- **Turning it off** puts the plan's own dimming values back. A change to dimming made in Windows' settings is followed.

**Where the power goes now**

On battery, the Saving tab shows the draw split into the CPU, which dynamic saving manages, and display and the rest.

**Brightness, measured**

The brightness experiment works again since the brightness fix in 0.24.1. The tip now shows this PC's own figure per 10% of brightness once a quick test has run.
