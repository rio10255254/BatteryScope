## A flatter layout: one place for each setting, at most one level down

Every page and menu was mapped. Some settings sat three or four levels deep (a menu inside a menu inside a row), some lived in up to four places, and the battery record could only be reached by clicking cards that looked like plain readouts. Now each setting has one home, on its tab or one level below it, and a choice among a few is a row of keys instead of a menu.

**Where things are now**
- **Performance:** the performance mode, CPU readings and Boost, the **GPU mode as keys** (Auto / Eco / Standard). Fan speeds are shown; their settings are one tap away on Hardware.
- **Saving:** every saving switch, and now the **Windows power mode for both power sources as keys** right on the tab (it used to be its own page, reached from four places). The header's power key opens this tab.
- **Battery:** the ranking. The right-hand card under the battery is now a clear **"Battery record ›"** entry showing health; the rate and remaining time share the left card.
- **Hardware:** **fans directly on the tab** (readings, presets, the curve editor), then **CPU power limit**, **charge limit 60 / 80 / 100%** and **keyboard backlight** as keys, Fn lock and display overdrive as switches, and the **ASUS keys flattened** (a switch, then the ROG key and Fn+C as keys — they were submenus inside a menu).
- **Settings:** a new **Diagnostics** section with stability and problem reports and the interface diagnostic.
- **⋯ menu:** dynamic saving, stability and problem reports, quit. **No submenus.** The ASUS mode is on Performance, the Windows power mode on Saving, CSV export and the share card on the battery record, the mini window and the diagnostic in Settings, protection mode and "record a problem" on the stability page.

**Removed duplicates:** the charge limit (battery record and Hardware → Hardware only), CSV export and share card (⋯ tools and battery record → battery record), mini window (⋯ tools and Settings → Settings), Windows power mode (⋯, header, Saving row, its own page → Saving).
