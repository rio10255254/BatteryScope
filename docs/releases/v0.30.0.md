## A new look: backlit keycaps and dot matrix

The macOS look and the old pixel style are gone. In their place is one style, designed from the reference clip: the panel is a small device that glows.

**Materials**

- **Keycaps.** Everything you can press is a matte keycap that stands up from the panel with a soft shadow: command rows, buttons, the three performance-mode keys and the tab keys. A key sinks a little when pressed.
- **Wells.** Readings sit in wells sunk into the panel: the battery display, LED meters and dot charts.
- **Indicator LEDs.** Status shows as small lights instead of coloured boxes. Attention is amber, a problem is red, and force-ending a program has the one red light.
- **Light from inside.** Only what is active is lit: the tab you are on, the chosen mode, a switch that is on, a selected program. Light spills onto the panel around it.

**Day and night**

- **Day.** Ivory keys with an amber backlight (core #F5C367).
- **Night.** Smoked keys with an ice-blue neon (white core, indigo halo).
- The light follows the theme (light or dark, or Windows' choice). The light-to-dark crossfade is kept.

**Dot matrix**

- Chinese and English use the embedded Cubic 11 dot-matrix font everywhere.
- Large numbers (power, battery %) are dot-matrix digits.
- The tab icons (bolt, leaf, battery, chip, flask, gear) and arrows are drawn from small square cells.
- Charts are columns of dots, with the newest column lit.

**Layout**

- **Header.** The name, the battery mascot when it is on, and a line on what this is. The power-mode key sits under the ⋯ and hide keys; it is lit while saving.
- **Tabs.** A row of six keys along the bottom of the panel, like the bottom row of a keyboard.
- **Pages.** Pages sit on the panel's face under their title. Sections have engraved labels.

**Motion (from the clip)**

- The light travels quickly to the new tab, mode or switch position, then blooms on the key it lands on, runs along its edge and settles.
- A changed value dissolves and re-forms.
- Everything stops when Windows reduces animations.

**Lighter to draw**

- Shadows and glows are worked out once per size and colour as small images, then only copied. There is no blur computed per frame.
- The partial repainting from 0.29 stays: animation repaints only what moves, and a page at rest repaints nothing.
- Windows contrast themes get system colours and outlines.

Removed: the Style and Frosted glass settings, the toolbar-and-segmented-tabs layout, the frosted glass and the macOS palette.
