## Performance modes change the power again, as they did with G-Helper

With CPU Boost off, the clock stops at the base clock. Every performance mode then draws about the same: on the G14, plugged in, 20 W at most in any mode, even at full load. G-Helper does more than switch the firmware mode. With each mode it also sets that mode's own CPU Boost and the Windows power mode: best efficiency for silent, balanced for balanced, best performance for turbo. BatteryScope only switched the firmware mode, so the modes looked alike.

**Mode link (plugged in)**

When you switch the performance mode while plugged in, BatteryScope now also sets the mode's CPU Boost and the Windows power mode. This covers the Perf page, Fn+F5 and the ROG key.

- **Starting values.** G-Helper's own settings for each mode (`auto_boost_N` and `powermode_N` in its config) when it has them. Otherwise:
  - Silent: Boost off
  - Balanced: Boost as your power plan has it now, so a Boost you switched off on purpose stays off
  - Turbo: Boost aggressive
- **Where to change it.** The "Mode link" row under the performance mode. It lets you pick each mode's Boost, leave the Windows power mode alone, turn the link off, or take G-Helper's settings again.
- **Battery is unchanged.** On battery, dynamic saving and the battery Boost switch keep handling these settings.
- **When it does not run.** It respects protection mode and the write guard around sleep and restarts, and stays out of the way of the control check and the lab.

Not measured on the G14 yet: how far turbo with Boost on now goes past balanced with Boost off, plugged into the 100 W USB-C charger.

建議：插電時切到 Turbo，CPU Boost 會自動打開、功耗可以拉高；切回平衡或靜音，Boost 回到關閉，又涼又安靜。每個模式的 Boost 可以在效能頁「模式連動」那一列調整。
