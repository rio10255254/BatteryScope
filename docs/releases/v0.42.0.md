## Every page on one screen: no scrolling

Every tab and page was measured against the room it has on the G14 (2880×1800 at 200%: 527 pixels between the header and the tab bar). Before, several pages ran two or three screens long. Now each one fits, without hiding anything: longer pages are split into **views** you switch between with a row of keys at the top, and the controls themselves take less room.

**How pages are laid out now**
- **Views instead of long pages.** Saving: *Switches / Goal / Analysis*. Battery record: *Last 7 days / By app*. Hardware: *Fans / Power / Keyboard*. Stability: *Overview / Next steps / Check / Report*. The new view slides in from the side it is on.
- **Tiles.** Switches and values sit two to a row as small tiles, a switch drawn in the tile or an arrow where it opens something. The Settings page is a single grid of tiles.
- **Keys with their name on the left.** A choice among a few (GPU mode, power limit, charge limit, keyboard backlight, ROG key, Fn+C, fan, editing mode, export) is one row: the name on the left, the keys on the right.
- **Performance** drops its section headings: mode keys, four readings (temperature, GHz, load, watts), the CPU chart with its power limit, Boost, GPU mode and a fan row.
- **Fan editor:** the point editor is one row (`< − °C + − % + >`), undo/redo/preset/reset/apply-to-all are one row of keys, and **the curve grows to fill whatever room is left**.
- **Lab:** starting a test is one row of keys (quick, full, single).
- **Stability:** the control check shows each result on one line; what each check found is folded underneath. When incident cards would not fit, the last ones are left out (they are also listed on the overview).

**Fixes found along the way**
- Notes, cards and check rows were measured at a slightly different width from the one they were drawn at, so a long English line could wrap once more than counted and its last line was cut off. They are now measured at the width they are drawn.
- Removed three functions the new layout left unused.

Every view measured (logical pixels, room 527): Performance 453; Saving 246 / 394 / 291; Battery record 423 / 321; Hardware 522 / 199 / 292; Lab 476; Settings 438; Stability 452 / 527 / 481 / 505. In English all fit except Stability → Next steps (555), which scrolls a little.
