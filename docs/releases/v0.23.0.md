## Stability monitor: blue screens, freezes and unexpected shutdowns, recorded and reported in one click

**What it records**
- **A black box.** Every 10 seconds it writes the state of the PC to disk, written through so a blue screen does not lose it: charge, power draw, CPU temperature and power, performance mode, fan and power presets, discrete GPU, refresh rate, activity, lab step and foreground app. BatteryScope also records every hardware write before making it, so a report shows whether BatteryScope had just changed something before a crash.
- **Windows' own records**, read after each start and every 15 minutes:
  - blue screens, with the stop code and its name;
  - unexpected shutdowns, a held power button, and an empty battery;
  - display driver resets;
  - hardware (WHEA) and disk errors.

  The records of one crash become one incident with the black box's last state before it.
- **Freezes.** With the screen on, a high-priority thread notices when the whole PC stops for 2.5 seconds or more. A hang of BatteryScope itself, or a crash, leaves a dump.

**What you see**
- A notification when a new blue screen, unexpected shutdown or freeze is found. The Perf tab shows a warning row when something happened in the last 7 days.
- A **Stability** page: counts for 30 days, each incident with its stop code name, where to look (storage, graphics, sleep and wake…) and the state at the time.

**Reporting**
- **Create a report**: a zip on the desktop with the summary, black box, event records, drivers and disks (SSD firmware included), Windows' reliability history, recent system errors and power settings. The summary is also copied, ready to paste.
- **Report with blue screen dumps**: also copies the latest minidumps, with administrator rights.
- **Mark a problem that just happened** (also in the ⋯ menu): marks a stutter or anything odd with the state before it.

Nothing is uploaded; the report stays on your PC until you send it.

建議：更新後照常使用。再遇到藍屏、自動重開或卡住時，打開「效能」首頁的穩定性提醒（或「⋯ → 穩定性與問題回報」），按「建立回報包」，把桌面上的 zip 或剪貼簿裡的摘要傳給開發者即可。
