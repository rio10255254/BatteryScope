## Hands off at risky moments, a protection mode, and a Stability page that reads at a glance

A second diagnosis of the G14's crashes found that most of them happened while the PC was going to sleep or restarting. In one of them, BatteryScope changed a power setting five seconds before the crash, as the PC entered sleep. BatteryScope cannot repair a hardware or driver fault, but it now makes sure it never acts at such a moment.

**Protection**
- **No writes during power transitions.**
  - While the PC goes to sleep, while the screen is off (how Modern Standby begins), and while it signs out, shuts down or restarts, every hardware and power-setting write is refused, down to the low-level ASUS ACPI and power-setting writers.
  - Automatic adjustments also wait after the lid closes (20 s), after a resume (10 s), after the screen comes back (5 s) and after BatteryScope starts (8 s, or 25 s right after Windows starts).
  - Refused writes are recorded in the black box.
- **Protection mode.** When BatteryScope finds a crash from the last 24 hours, it switches to watching only. Dynamic saving, the automatic refresh rate and GPU mode, the low-battery switch, keyboard dimming, experiments and re-applying ASUS settings all pause until you choose "Resume automatic adjustments". You can also turn it on yourself from the Stability page or the ⋯ menu.
- **A careful start.** Nothing is written at the moment BatteryScope starts. It reads Windows' crash records first, then restores what a crash left changed, and only then applies the remembered ASUS settings.
- **G-Helper alongside.** "Next steps" warns when G-Helper is running (both programs drive the same firmware interface), or when its settings undervolt the CPU or overclock the GPU.

**The Stability page, redesigned**
- One banner on the state of things: all is well, watching, how many incidents in 7 days, or protection mode. The home page shows it too when there is something to say.
- A 30-day timeline, one cell per day, red for a crash.
- "Next steps" as a checklist. What BatteryScope can see for itself is ticked off: three clean sleep-and-wake cycles, one clean restart, three days without a crash.
- Each incident as a card: what it means in plain words, when ("yesterday 02:17"), and the state of the PC at the time.

**Fixed**
- A timing race could report "BatteryScope stopped responding" (and save a dump) when the panel was fine.
- Time the monitor spent reading Windows' records could be counted as a system freeze.

建議：更新後如果 24 小時內當過機，會自動進入保護模式——這是正常的。照「穩定性」頁的「下一步」做（移除 NI 驅動、G-Helper 降壓歸零、睡眠測試 3 次），都打勾、幾天沒事後再按「恢復自動調整」。
