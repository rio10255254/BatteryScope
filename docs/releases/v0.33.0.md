## A neutral day theme, readable small text, glows without bands, liquid motion

From a screenshot of 0.32 on the G14 and the notes that came with it.

- **Day theme.** The panel is a cool, neutral grey instead of ivory, and the keys are nearly white. The amber light now reads as light, not as the panel's own colour.
- **Small text.** Secondary text is darker by day and brighter by night.
- **Glows without bands.** A soft glow used to show its 8-bit steps as bands of colour. Sprites are now worked out in floating point and rounded once with a little ordered noise, and light falls off with a longer, fainter tail. The tab bar is a little taller so the open tab's light has room above it.
- **Liquid motion** (after the way selection moves in iOS liquid glass).
  - The lit key moves between tabs and between performance modes like a drop: its leading edge darts ahead, its trailing edge clings and follows late, and it is pulled into a thin capsule between them.
  - Arriving, it is squashed wide and wobbles back to its shape.
- **Closer to the reference clip.**
  - A lit key is frosted glass: lighter, a little blue by night, with its light spread wide and thin over the panel instead of hugging the key.
  - A thin neon line rests on the lit key's edge, and runs round to it when the key arrives.
  - The opened tab's icon gathers from scattered dots, as the sun and moon do in the clip.
- **Springs.** Blocks of a page land a little past their place and come back. Switch keys overshoot slightly.
- **More kinds of motion.** As a page opens, the switches that are on flick on one after another. A row swells up under the pointer like a bubble.
