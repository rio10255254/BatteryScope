## Charts instead of paragraphs, a simpler menu, one place for updates, and a smarter dynamic saving

**Charts.** The pages that were walls of text are now charts that update live:
- **Power overview:** your last 30 minutes in use and while still against the goal, a live battery power chart, the state of each setting at a glance, the ways to save ranked by watts (measured or estimated), the programs using power, and what dynamic saving sees.
- **Power lab:** what each setting saves, as bars with their 95% range, plus where battery power goes and detection accuracy.
- **Programs using power:** now, since BatteryScope started, and background programs worth a look.
- **Battery & history:** charge, health and cycles, capacity, and the last 7 days.

**A simpler menu.** Duplicates are merged:
- Dynamic saving status and the power goal are now the **power overview**.
- Session energy and power-hungry background apps are now **programs using power**.
- Battery details and history are now **battery & history**.
- The lab is quick test, full test, results and export, with the rest under Advanced.
- In Settings, the battery target is folded into the power goal (dynamic saving now saves harder whenever you are over the goal), and the performance list is folded into app rules. Program details now has a single "Rule" button.

**Updates in one place.** Settings → Update shows your version and opens one menu: check or install now, and choose between installing automatically (the default, waiting for any running test), notifying, or not checking. When a new version is found, "Install update" appears at the top of the ⋯ menu.

**Dynamic saving, tuned on real data.** The first real test showed that efficiency mode cost only milliseconds and that CPU Boost cost about 5 W. So:
- Everything except bursts, heavy work and calls now runs in efficiency with Boost off.
- A new **burst** state (input together with a CPU spike, such as a program opening or a page loading) gets a short balanced boost.
- Heavy work uses balanced instead of best performance; the lab's measurements can still change that.
- Low battery saves one level harder; very low battery saves two.
- Being over the power goal saves one level harder, and background apps get efficiency mode twice as fast then.
- A measured cost of efficiency falls back to balanced instead of your original mode.
- Browsing and typing results now also tune the "in use" state.
