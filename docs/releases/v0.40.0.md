## A more complete, more precise fan curve editor

The custom curves from 0.39 can now be shaped the way G-Helper's can, with more ways to be exact.

- **Dragging:** the values ride beside the point while you drag it ("65° · 25%"). Hold **Shift** while dragging for a quarter of the movement: fine steps without leaving the mouse. Where the curve's rules stop a point (its neighbours, the safety floor), it stops under the pointer too instead of drifting.
- **Hover:** a point lights up under the pointer and the cursor becomes a move cursor; anywhere else on the chart a guide reads off the speed the curve gives at that temperature ("70° · 38%").
- **Exact steps:** a point editor under the chart: < and > choose the point, − and + move its temperature by 1 °C and its speed by 1%, and keep stepping while held.
- **Undo and redo:** "Undo" appears after a change (Ctrl+Z), "Redo" after an undo (Ctrl+Y); they reach back over drags, steps, resets, presets and "use for all three modes", and show the fan and mode they change.
- **Start from a preset:** Quiet, Standard or Strong copied into the curve being edited.
- **Comparison:** the other fan's curve is drawn faintly behind, labelled.
- **Confirmation:** after a write the readout says whether the firmware took it ("written" / "refused by the firmware").
- **Larger, finer chart:** taller plot, fine grid lines every 5 °C and 10%. A curve that changes a lot (another fan or mode, a reset, a preset) glides to its new shape.
