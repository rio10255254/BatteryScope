## Power lab: measure where the power goes, and what each setting really saves

BatteryScope 0.13.0 adds an experimental **Power lab** (⋯ → Power lab), the first step toward dynamic power management tuned to this PC. Nothing changes automatically yet: this release only measures.

- **Record detailed data** every 2 seconds: battery, CPU package and core power, and their difference (display, discrete GPU, and the rest), plus what you are doing (idle, interactive, video or music, calls, heavy work, full-screen games), the foreground app, the refresh rate, whether the discrete GPU is awake and which apps use it, compositor dropped frames, the power plan's CPU Boost and energy preference, and G-Helper's state. Daily CSV files stay in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BatteryScope\lab`.
- **Power by activity:** see how the average battery power splits for each activity, and how often the discrete GPU is awake.
- **A/B experiments** for CPU Boost, processor energy preference, refresh rate, and power mode: on battery the setting alternates every 2 minutes in randomized pairs, blocks changed by another program (such as G-Helper) are repeated, and after 8 pairs the setting is restored and the paired difference in power and dropped frames is shown with a 95% range.

Everything is restored when you stop, plug in, quit, or after a crash. Not yet verified on real hardware: whether a standard user can change CPU Boost and energy preference in the power plan, how meaningful the compositor's dropped-frame counts are, and how G-Helper interacts.

如果你已經裝了 0.12.0，會收到更新通知，點一下即可自動更新。新功能在「⋯ → 省電實驗室」：開啟「記錄詳細資料」後照平常用電池使用，就能看到耗電花在哪裡；再選一項實驗，讓它用電池時跑約 32 分鐘，就會得到該設定在你這台電腦上實際省多少電、會不會掉格。所有資料只存在本機。
