## One place for everything, and motion that answers you

The panel had grown the same switch in three or four places. This version gives every control one home, and tidies how the pages move and respond.

**Layout**

- **Perf** (home): the performance mode, CPU, GPU, fans and the power limit. The quick-switch block is gone; CPU Boost sits with the CPU.
- **Saving**: every saving switch, grouped into automatic saving, screen and devices, and goal and rules. "Switch refresh rate dynamically" moved here from Settings.
- **Battery**: the ranking, then health, the last seven days and what has added up. The second "using power now" list is gone, since the ranking is that list.
- **ASUS**: the charge limit, keyboard, lighting and ASUS software. Mode, fans and temperatures are on Perf only.
- **Lab**: start a test, the results, then the data.
- **Settings**: reminders, looks and general options only, drawn like every other page, with real switches.
- Long explanations fold away under a row such as "How dynamic saving works" and open when clicked.

**Motion and feedback**

- A page slides in from the side its tab is on; a page deeper in a tab slides in from the right and back out to the left.
- A value that changes glows for a moment, so a click is seen to land.
- Pointing at a chart lights the column and shows its value.
- A page longer than the view fades at the edge where it continues.
- Folds unroll and roll up; all motion stops when Windows is set to reduce animations.

Checked with screenshots and the panel test under Wine; the motion itself was not watched on a real screen.
