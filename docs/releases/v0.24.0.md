## Control check: which controls really work on this PC

The G14 showed that a write that returns no error is not proof that anything changed: its panel ignored the brightness call. Several ASUS controls cannot be read back at all, so until now BatteryScope took the firmware's word for them.

**"Check the controls" on the Stability page** tests each one on your PC, in about 40 seconds:

- **Refresh rate, Windows power mode, CPU Boost, energy preference, brightness:** another value is written, read back, and the original restored.
- **ASUS performance mode:** a few seconds of all-core load in silent and in turbo, comparing CPU power.
- **CPU power limit:** the same load at the 15 W preset must draw clearly less.
- **Fan curve and keyboard backlight:** accepted by the firmware with nothing to read back. They are marked "Write only", never "Works".
- **GPU Eco and charge limit:** not toggled. The page says how each is confirmed in use.

Each row shows what was seen, for example "silent 31.5 W, turbo 62.0 W". Results are copied to the clipboard and included in reports. Automatic tests skip a setting found to have no effect.

Also: the brightness setting is read back up to three times before it is judged, since the level can take a moment to arrive.

建議：更新後到「穩定性」頁最下方按「檢查每項控制是否真的有作用」（插電時做，約 40 秒，風扇會轉幾秒），再把自動複製的結果貼給開發者。保護模式開著也可以檢查。
