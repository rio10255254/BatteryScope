## Fixes for 0.33 on the real machine: steps in the glow, stiff and stuttering motion

0.33 still showed steps in the glow on the G14, and its motion stuttered. Both came from how 0.33 was built.

- **Steps in the glow.** The lit key's light fell across two colours at once, the tray and the panel, so the tray's edge showed through the light as a step. The tabs and the performance modes no longer sit in a filled tray (a dotted line above the tabs, a thin outline round the modes), and the light is a little tighter so it is not squeezed by the edge above the tabs.
- **Stutter.**
  - Each frame of the liquid move made a new key bitmap. The moving key is now the resting bitmap stretched: three blends a frame.
  - Opening a page repainted the whole page every frame for over half a second. The page is now painted once and that picture slides and fades in; its digits, meters and switches come alive after it lands.
  - A row under the pointer changed size every frame. It only fades in now.

這些修正是依原因推斷的，我這邊無法重現實機的掉格；請在 G14 上再看一次，並告訴我哪個動作還是不順。
