## A slower, fuller light when the performance mode changes

- **It takes its time.** Changing the performance mode by hand now plays for about two seconds (silent 2.1 s, balanced 1.9 s, turbo 1.7 s) instead of 0.7–0.9 s.
- **It goes round the whole screen, every mode.** Two lights leave the bottom centre, gather speed up the sides and ease into each other at the top centre; the border fills softly behind them. Where they meet the light blooms, an echo runs back down both sides, and the whole border breathes once and sinks back into the edge. Before, silent lit only the bottom and balanced stopped part way.
- **Each mode has its own character.** Silent is cool blue, calm and long-tailed; balanced is white; turbo is warm orange, sets off sooner, burns hotter and sends a stronger echo.
- **More visible.** The glow reaches about a quarter further into the screen and swells where the light is strongest, then draws back to the edge as it fades instead of only going transparent.
- **Smoother.** Every movement starts and lands without a jolt; the faint outer glow is dithered so it falls off without visible bands; the light turns the screen's corners without a seam.
- **Lighter to draw.** The border light was redrawn to touch memory in order, skip the dark stretches, update only the band that changed and compute the soft outer glow once per 2×2 block. On the test machine a frame of the new, larger effect costs at most about 5 ms at 2880 × 1800, against 7–9 ms for the old, smaller one.
- The plug-in light's moving front is feathered instead of cut off.
