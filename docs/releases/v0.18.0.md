## A new layout with tabs, ASUS hardware control without G-Helper, and one owner per setting

**Tabs.** The panel now has tabs along the bottom:
- **Apps**
- **Saving**
- **Battery**
- **ASUS** (on ASUS laptops)
- **Lab**
- **Setup**

Each tab is one page: live charts on top, and its controls as rows you click (or choose with the arrows and Enter). Rows switch a setting or open its choices in place, in the same pixel style. The ⋯ menu keeps only quick switches and tools. While dynamic saving runs, the header pill shows what it detects, and clicking it opens the Saving tab.

**No more conflicts.** Each setting has one owner at a time:
- **The lab** while it measures.
- **Your manual choice** of a battery power mode or ASUS mode, for this battery session. You can hand it back from the Saving tab, the ⋯ menu, or the power mode page.
- **Dynamic saving**, following app rules. It also takes over the performance list and low-battery saving.

Programs you set to efficiency mode yourself are left alone. The saving tips no longer suggest what dynamic saving already does.

**More ASUS control:**
- **Automatic GPU:** Eco on battery and standard when plugged in. It waits until no program uses the discrete GPU.
- **Charge limit:** re-applied after a restart or sleep.
- **Keyboard backlight:** four levels, plus auto-off after 30 s idle on battery.
- **ASUS keys:** BatteryScope can handle the backlight keys, Fn+F5 and the ROG key.
- **ASUS services:** stop and disable all ASUS and Armoury Crate services in one step, or restore them.


**⋯ → ASUS hardware** (ASUS laptops) talks to the firmware through the ASUS ATKACPI driver (ASUS System Control Interface), the interface Armoury Crate and G-Helper use:

- **Status page (charts):** model, performance mode, GPU Eco, display output, charge limit, overdrive, temperatures, fan speeds, and power limits.
- **Controls:** performance mode (silent, balanced, turbo); GPU Eco or standard, with a warning if programs are using the discrete GPU; charge limit 60, 80, or 100%; and screen overdrive. Every change is read back.
- **Dynamic saving** now also sets the ASUS performance mode:
  - silent for light use;
  - balanced while opening or loading, in calls, and for heavy work;
  - restored in games.
  - Changes from Fn keys or other programs are respected for 10 minutes.
- **The lab** measures what silent mode saves: two steps in the quick test, three in the full test. These steps are skipped on other PCs.
- `BatteryScope.exe --asus-dump` prints every firmware value read, to confirm them on a new model.

Not yet verified on real hardware. The device numbers come from the Linux kernel's documentation of the same interface. Please run `--asus-dump` once, or open the status page, and check the values against G-Helper or Armoury Crate. Fan curves and power-limit changes will come after that.

建議：更新後開「⋯ → 華碩硬體」看讀到的值是否和 G-Helper 一致；一致的話可以完全關掉 G-Helper，由動態省電接手效能模式。
