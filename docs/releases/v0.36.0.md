## The last three items of the 0.34 design

- **CPU readings in three small wells.** The Performance page shows temperature, clock and load side by side, each in its own well with the value at twice the size, instead of one line of small text. A reading that is not available shows a dash; a hot CPU lights an indicator beside the label.
- **The ranking's buttons appear only when a program is selected**, and only the ones that apply to it: details, close (it has a window), stop service (it hosts one that can stop) and force quit. With nothing selected the list uses the space. Up to three buttons share one row; four take two.
- **One 16-point margin everywhere.** The header, the ranking, the chart pages, the tab strip and the notifications now share the same left and right edge (they were 18, 14 and 16 before).
